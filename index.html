Why compare before you book?

The same hotel room can cost a different amount on different booking sites — sometimes by a lot. Prices change with demand, dates and special offers, and one site rarely has the lowest price every time. Comparing takes a minute and can save real money on every trip, whether it is a budget weekend or a luxury stay.

HotelsComparateur brings the comparison to one place: search once, see the options, and choose the deal that fits your trip and your budget.

Frequently asked questions

Is HotelsComparateur free to use?

Yes. Searching and comparing hotel prices here is completely free. You only pay the hotel or booking site when you book, and the price you see is the price they charge.

How does HotelsComparateur make money?

When you click through to a trusted booking partner and complete a booking, we may earn a small commission from that partner at no extra cost to you. That is how the site stays free.

Do I book directly with the hotel?

You book through one of our trusted booking partners, such as Booking.com, Expedia or Hotels.com. Your reservation, payment and confirmation are handled by that partner under their terms.

Can I find cheap hotels as well as luxury hotels?

Yes. Compare budget stays, mid-range hotels, boutique hotels and luxury stays side by side, and filter by what matters to you — price, location, rating or amenities.

When is the best time to book a hotel?

Prices move with demand, so comparing early usually helps. For popular cities and peak dates, booking several weeks ahead tends to give more choice; for flexible trips, last-minute deals can appear too.

Which destinations can I search?

You can search hotels in more than 200 countries. Popular starting points are Paris, New York, Tokyo, London, Las Vegas and Dubai — each with its own guide on this site.
