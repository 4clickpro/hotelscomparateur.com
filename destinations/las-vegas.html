Where to stay in Las Vegas

The Strip is where most visitors stay — the big resorts, the shows and the nightlife are all there, and the centre of the Strip (around Bellagio and Caesars) is the most walkable stretch. Downtown's Fremont Street is cheaper and more compact, with a classic Vegas feel. Off-Strip hotels are usually the best value of all, especially if you have a car.

Tips for finding a good Las Vegas hotel deal

  • Compare the same dates everywhere. The same room can cost a different amount on different booking sites.
  • Stay midweek. Sunday to Thursday nights are usually far cheaper than Friday and Saturday.
  • Always add the resort fee. Compare the total price including the nightly fee, not just the room rate.
  • Check the convention calendar. Big events can double or triple Strip prices overnight.
  • Look off-Strip for value. A short drive or rideshare can save a lot per night.

Las Vegas hotel questions

Is it better to stay on the Strip or downtown?

The Strip keeps you in the middle of the resorts, shows and nightlife. Downtown (Fremont Street) is cheaper, more compact and has a classic Vegas feel. Off-Strip hotels are usually the best value if you have a car.

When are Las Vegas hotels cheapest?

Weekday nights (Sunday to Thursday) are usually far cheaper than Friday and Saturday. Summer heat brings lower rates, while big conventions, fights and New Year push prices up.

Do Las Vegas hotels charge resort fees?

Yes — almost all Strip and downtown hotels add a nightly resort fee on top of the room rate, usually covering Wi-Fi, pool and gym access. Always check the total price including the fee before you compare.
