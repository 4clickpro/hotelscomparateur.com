Where to stay in Santorini

Oia is the postcard choice — caldera-view suites, cave rooms and sunset terraces, at the island's highest prices. Fira, the capital, is the practical base: more hotels, more restaurants, buses to everywhere, and caldera views at lower rates than Oia. Imerovigli, between Fira and Oia, is the quiet luxury pick — high-end suites with the same caldera view and fewer crowds. For the beach, Kamari and Perissa on the south-east coast offer black-sand beaches and better value, and the island's wineries and Akrotiri suit a slower, second-day trip.

Tips for finding a good Santorini hotel deal

  • Compare the same dates everywhere. The same room can cost a different amount on different booking sites.
  • Travel in the shoulder season. April, May, October and early November are usually the cheapest months to stay, with the caldera just as good.
  • Check what the view actually is. "Caldera view" and "sea view" are different rooms at different prices — check the room description before booking.
  • Stay near the bus route. The bus from Fira reaches Oia, the beaches and Akrotiri cheaply, so a hotel a short walk from a stop saves on taxis.
  • Book early for summer. June to September sells out fast at the best caldera hotels, especially suites with a private plunge pool.

Santorini hotel questions

What is the best area to stay in Santorini?

Oia is the classic choice for caldera views and sunset suites, and it carries the highest prices. Fira is the island hub, with more choice, restaurants and nightlife. Imerovigli is the quiet, high-end pick between the two, while Kamari and Perissa suit beach stays at lower rates.

When is the cheapest time to visit Santorini?

April, May, October and early November are usually the quietest months, with lower hotel prices than the June to September peak. Late spring and early autumn offer the best balance of weather and rates.

How far ahead should I book a Santorini hotel?

For the best choice of caldera-view rooms, book several months ahead, especially for summer and honeymoon dates. Comparing prices across booking sites first usually finds a better rate than booking the first price you see.
