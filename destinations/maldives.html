Where to stay in the Maldives

Most Maldives stays are resort islands, one resort per island, so the choice is the island, not the neighbourhood. Resorts close to Male are reached by speedboat, which runs day and night. Resorts farther out need a seaplane, which only flies in daylight — so a late arrival means a night near the airport first. North Male Atoll suits a first trip, with the shortest transfers. Baa Atoll is the pick for diving and the manta season, and the southern atolls suit travellers who want a quieter, longer stay. Overwater villas are the classic luxury choice; beach villas with a private stretch of sand usually cost less for the same resort.

Tips for finding a good Maldives resort deal

  • Compare the same dates everywhere. The same villa can cost a different amount on different booking sites — and the board basis matters: room only, half board and all-inclusive are different prices for the same room.
  • Count the transfer. A seaplane transfer is charged per person on top of the room, so add it before comparing resorts.
  • Travel in the low season for the lowest rates. May to October is usually the cheapest time to stay, with warm seas even when it rains.
  • Check what is included. Meals, drinks, snorkelling gear and airport transfers vary by rate — compare the full package, not just the room.
  • Book early for the dry season. November to April sells out fast at the best resorts, especially overwater villas.

Maldives hotel questions

What is the best way to choose a Maldives resort?

Start with the transfer: resorts near Male are reached by speedboat, while farther atolls need a seaplane, which adds cost and only runs in daylight. Then compare the same room and board basis — room only, half board or all-inclusive — across booking sites, because the same villa can be listed very differently.

When is the best time to visit the Maldives?

November to April is the dry season, with the sunniest weather and the highest prices. May to October is the low season, with lower rates and a higher chance of rain, but still warm seas and good diving.

How far ahead should I book a Maldives resort?

For the best choice of villas, book several months ahead, especially for the dry season and honeymoon dates. Comparing prices across booking sites first usually finds a better rate than booking the first price you see.
