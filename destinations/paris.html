Where to stay in Paris

Paris rewards staying central. The 1st arrondissement puts you between the Louvre and the Seine; the 4th (Le Marais) is best for boutique hotels, cafés and shopping; the 7th is the quiet, elegant choice near the Eiffel Tower; Saint-Germain (6th) is classic Paris — bookshops, bistros and art galleries; and Montmartre (18th) trades centrality for village streets and the best views in the city.

Tips for finding a good Paris hotel deal

  • Compare the same dates everywhere. The same room can cost a different amount on different booking sites.
  • Travel off-peak. January, February and November are usually the cheapest months to stay.
  • Check the metro map. A hotel two stops out can cost far less and still be ten minutes from the sights.
  • Watch for city tax. Paris charges a nightly tourist tax per person, on top of the room rate.
  • Book refundable when plans are loose. Flexible rates cost a little more but let you change dates free.

Paris hotel questions

What is the best area to stay in Paris?

First-time visitors often pick the 1st, 4th or 7th arrondissements for sightseeing on foot. Le Marais suits boutique stays and food lovers, Saint-Germain suits a classic Paris feel, and Montmartre suits village charm with views.

When is the cheapest time to visit Paris?

January, February and November are usually the quietest months, with lower hotel prices. Spring and autumn offer the best balance of weather and rates, while summer and December are the busiest.

How far ahead should I book a Paris hotel?

For the best choice, book several weeks ahead, especially for spring and summer. Comparing prices across booking sites first usually finds a better rate than booking the first price you see.
